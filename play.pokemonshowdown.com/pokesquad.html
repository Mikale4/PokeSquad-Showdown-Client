PokeSquad Battler

Battle with PokeSquad Battler's own rules: pick the [Gen 9] PokeSquad format. Three Pokémon on the field a side, a turn meter, three moves each (one per slot: S1 has no cooldown, S2 and S3 do), Lv 100.

Choose a name (no password needed). On the same Wi-Fi, challenge each other: the ladder never pairs two players from one connection.
